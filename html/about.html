Get to know

Mark & Angie Gray

Behind MGBD Services are Mark and Angie, classic Rover enthusiasts with decades of experience owning, driving, repairing and restoring Rover P6s. Their passion and hands-on experience remain at the heart of the business today.

For Mark and Angie, the Rover P6 has always been more than just a car. Over the years they’ve owned a number of memorable classics, attended and supported Rover events, and become part of the wider community of owners and enthusiasts. Their collection has even included VVC 700S, the very last Rover P6 to leave the production line, which MGBD has owned since 2005. It’s this personal enthusiasm for the cars, combined with years of practical experience, that remains at the heart of MGBD Services.

About the Business

MGBD Services is a specialist Rover P6 business with over 32 years of experience owning, repairing, restoring and running these classic cars. Based in Staffordshire, the business has grown from a passion for the Rover P6 into a specialist resource for owners and enthusiasts.

At the heart of MGBD is an extensive range of new, used and refurbished Rover P6 parts, helping owners find everything from everyday replacement components to increasingly difficult-to-source items. When original parts are no longer readily available, MGBD’s specialist services provide further options through machining and sleeving, repairing, refurbishing, manufacturing, chroming and polishing. This combination of parts and specialist services allows the business to help owners preserve original components wherever possible while also providing solutions when replacements are hard to find.

MGBD’s Staffordshire premises also include a dedicated workshop, where servicing, repairs and restoration work can be carried out on classic Rovers and certain other classic vehicles. Having the parts operation and workshop together means decades of hands-on Rover knowledge can be applied directly to the cars and components that come through the business. From helping source a hard-to-find part to carrying out more involved workshop work, MGBD continues to provide practical support for owners who want to maintain, repair and preserve their classic Rovers for years to come.
