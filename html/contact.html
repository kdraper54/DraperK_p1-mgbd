faq's

What information should I provide when enquiring about a part?

Provide as much information as possible about the part you require and the Rover it is for. Details such as the model, year, engine and any relevant part information can help MGBD understand exactly what you need.

What happens if the part I need is no longer available?

MGBD offers new, used and refurbished Rover P6 parts, along with specialist services including repairing, refurbishing, machining and manufacturing. If the part you need is difficult to find, get in touch to discuss your requirements and see how MGBD may be able to help.

Do you only specialize in the Rover P6?

The Rover P6 is MGBD’s main speciality, backed by over 32 years of experience owning, repairing, restoring and running these cars. However, their workshop also provides servicing and repairs for certain other classic vehicles.

Can I discuss my Rover with you if I’m unsure what it needs?

Yes. MGBD encourages customers to get in touch and discuss their requirements. With decades of hands-on Rover P6 experience, they can help you determine what parts, repairs or specialist services may be suitable for your car.

I’m thinking of buying a Rover P6. What should I look for?

There are several important areas to inspect, including the body structure, engine, transmission, steering and suspension, brakes, trim and electrics. Our Rover P6 Buyer’s Guide provides more information on the common problems and warning signs to look out for before purchasing.
